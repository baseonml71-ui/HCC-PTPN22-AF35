# Project overview

PTPN22 is the biological anchor; AF35 is a distributed transcriptional state readout. The frozen scientific authority is JITC_MAIN_v3, identified by checksums in provenance/SCIENTIFIC_AUTHORITY.tsv.

The analyses connect patient-resolved T-cell state architecture, clonotype context, conditional spatial organization and pretreatment clinical association. Patients, donors or independent experiments are the biological replicates; cells, clones, spots and regions are nested observations.

PTPN22-associated activation-feedback biology is supported by HCC mouse functional evidence, human HCC peripheral-blood proximal signaling and molecular feedback studies. These sources do not establish PTPN22 control of all AF35 members or a therapeutic direction. See the manuscript references: Cubas et al., doi:10.1136/jitc-2020-001439; Ho et al., doi:10.1172/JCI146950; Yang et al., doi:10.1126/sciadv.adw2568.
